How to read this

SHORT and LONG are directional. SELL VOL and BUY VOL are not — they say the option market has priced the event richly or cheaply, nothing about which way it resolves.

The small label under each score is what a backtest actually found. Only dilution-short is supported: across 5,379 point-in-time filing observations, cash runway under six months ran −5.4% abnormal at 21 days, and −27.7% at 126 days when the company is also a serial issuer. Run-up fade, trial design and insider activity were tested and did not hold up — they are shown with that label rather than deleted. Both volatility calls are untested: there is no free source of historical option chains.

Most dates are a window, not a day. A trial's primary completion date is when the last patient reaches the endpoint, not when the company announces — only 3.3% of big moves land within five days of it, against 5.2% from chance. Windows narrow to guided where a company has stated timing in a filing. Only PDUFA dates are hard.
