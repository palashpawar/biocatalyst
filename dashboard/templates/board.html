What the verdicts mean. SHORT and LONG are directional. SELL VOL / BUY VOL are not — they are a view on whether the option market has priced the event richly or cheaply, not on which way it resolves. Always read the verdict together with its evidence badge: only dilution-short is supported (cash runway under six months ran −5.4% abnormal at 21 days and −16.2% at 126 days across 5,379 point-in-time filing observations, both surviving multiple testing). Run-up fade tested as not supported, and both volatility verdicts are untested — there is no free source of historical option chains. The News column is context only and never moves a verdict: free news sources have no point-in-time archive, so a sentiment rule cannot be backtested yet. Scores are logged daily so that it becomes testable once enough forward history exists. Read thin scores with care — they come from fewer than four scored headlines. The direction of a binary readout itself is not forecastable from this data. Ticker links open Robinhood.
